## Brand & Style

This design system expresses cozy minimalism with tactile physical cues, engineered specifically for modern couples navigating misaligned, non-traditional schedules. Rather than evoking corporate calendar tension or cold productivity metrics, the aesthetic radiates safety, togetherness, and clarity. 

The interface feels like smooth, hand-finished stone and creamy cardstock:
- **Atmospheric Warmth:** Soft off-white canvases paired with high-clarity typography eliminate visual exhaustion.
- **Tactile Comfort:** Generous corner radii, pill toggles, and layered cards provide physical satisfaction under the thumb.
- **Relational Resonance:** Schedule overlap is treated not as mere empty space, but as a celebrated discovery—the "Golden Window." Visual cues highlight mutual availability with bright emeralds and luminous honey accents.
- **Mobile Ergonomics:** Strict adherence to 48px+ touch targets, natural bottom-sheet reachability, and persistent thumb-zone navigation for seamless one-handed use on mobile devices.

## Colors

The palette balances serene domestic calm with vibrant semantic signaling across partners' daily blocks. The system operates primarily in a warm light mode, prioritizing optical contrast and legibility under direct sunlight.

### Core Canvas & Neutrals
- **Canvas Base:** `#FBFBFA` (warm soft stone) delivers an organic foundation, preventing glare.
- **Surface Elevation 1 (Cards):** `#FFFFFF` pure crisp white creates clear layer separation.
- **Surface Elevation 2 (Sub-panels):** `#F4F4F0` for interior grouping wells and inset slots.
- **Text Primary:** `#0F172A` (deep slate) provides optimal contrast.
- **Text Secondary:** `#475569` for auxiliary details and partner time labels.
- **Borders & Dividers:** `#E2E8F0` with subtle warm undertones to delineate schedule lanes without visual clutter.

### Semantic Categories
- **Shared / Golden Free Time (Primary):** `#059669` (core emerald), `#10B981` (hover/interactive), with `#ECFDF5` as the soft ambient fill. Indicates mutual freedom and spontaneous date opportunities.
- **College / Study (Secondary):** `#D97706` (deep honey), `#F59E0B` (luminous amber), `#FFFBEB` (tint container). Denotes classes, lectures, and focused academic time.
- **Work / Shifts (Tertiary):** `#4F46E5` (calm indigo), `#3B82F6` (slate blue accent), `#EEF2FF` (tint container). Maps professional shift commitments and commutes.
- **Busy / Solitary Commitment:** `#E11D48` (dusky rose), `#F43F5E` (punch rose), `#FFF1F2` (tint container). Highlights non-negotiable personal obligations or individual appointments.
- **Golden Synergy Highlight:** `#B45309` text against `#FEF3C7` container with `#F59E0B` micro-borders, reserved for calculated overlap periods ("Golden Windows").

## Typography

The pairing combines the geometric warmth of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for structural elements and titles with the precision of **Inter** for data grids, shift hours, and micro-labels.

### Structural Typographic Rules
- **Headline Rhythm:** `Plus Jakarta Sans` brings friendly curves to section headers, partner names, and celebration alerts.
- **Tabular Alignment:** All shift times, overlap calculations, and hours must utilize `font-feature-settings: "tnum"` (tabular figures) within `Inter` to prevent timeline jitter during view scrubbing.
- **Micro-Badges:** All status tags under 12px must enforce uppercase styling with +0.04em tracking for instant glanceability on high-DPI screens.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built for mobile viewports, scaling up cleanly to tablets and desktop side-by-side split screens.

### Spatial Architecture
- **Mobile First Framework:** Fixed viewport canvas with zero horizontal scroll. Safe-area top padding handles dynamic notches; safe-area bottom provides room for floating bottom-bar navigation.
- **Touch Target Integrity:** Every clickable card, chip, date tab, or slot selector enforces a strict minimum footprint of 48px × 48px, padded internally when visual tokens are compact.
- **Dual-User Timeline Grid:** Dual parallel columns (`gutter: 0.75rem`) split schedules cleanly between Partner A and Partner B, bridging in the center with a mutual overlap rail.

### Breakpoints & Reflow
- **Mobile (<640px):** Single-column stack with top-anchored partner toggle or synchronized 2-track split view with 12px lateral padding.
- **Tablet (640px - 1024px):** Synchronized week-view planner. Both partners' full-week columns sit side-by-side with 16px margins.
- **Desktop (>1024px):** Centered max-width shell (980px) flanked by ambient free-time analytics and calendar integration widgets.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through tactile soft-layering: low-intensity, multi-stop ambient shadows combined with gentle interior borders. This avoids flat abstraction while steering clear of heavy skeuomorphic clutter.

### Elevation Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Flat Canvas):** `#FBFBFA` flat background without shadow.
- **Level 1 (Card & Slot Tiles):** `#FFFFFF` surface resting on a dual-shadow system:
  `box-shadow: 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04);`
  Enclosed by a subtle border: `1px solid rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.8)`.
- **Level 2 (Active Shifts & In-Focus Cards):**
  `box-shadow: 0 8px 20px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03);`
  Creates a distinct lift when dragging or tapping an event.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Overlays & Sticky Synergy Bar):**
  `box-shadow: 0 20px 32px -8px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12), 0 8px 16px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04);`
  Accompanied by `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` for sticky contextual notifications.

### Golden Synergy Glow
For confirmed "Golden Windows," replace standard slate shadows with an amber-emerald hybrid glow:
`box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.25), 0 1px 4px 0 rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.15);`

## Shapes

The design uses pill-shaped and pebble-soft geometry to create an inviting, approachable feel.

- **Pill Primitives (`rounded-full`):** Category badges, overlap indicators, floating action buttons, and day-picker chips.
- **Card Surfaces (`rounded-2xl` to `rounded-3xl` / 1.5rem - 2rem):** Shift blocks, partner comparison panels, and bottom sheets carry generous corner curves, removing sharp mechanical edges.
- **Nested Insets:** Internal schedule blocks adopt nested radii calculated by: `Outer Radius - Padding` (e.g., a card with 24px radius and 8px padding holds a 16px radius event block), preserving proportional rhythm.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary Action (Fill):** Height 48px minimum. Rounded pill shape (`rounded-full`). Background `#059669` transitioning to `#047857` on active tap. Bold label typography in pure white, accompanied by subtle active scale down (`transform: scale(0.98)`).
- **Secondary (Tactile Ghost):** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1.5px solid #E2E8F0`, text `#0F172A`. Deep active press state `#F4F4F0`.
- **Icon Buttons:** 48px square touch perimeter with centered 20px glyphs, housed in 40px rounded pebble wrappers.

### Shift & Event Cards
- Inset within calendar columns. Left edge features a 4px solid vertical bar in the semantic status color.
- Card fill uses the corresponding semantic soft-tint (e.g., `#ECFDF5` for free windows, `#EEF2FF` for shifts).
- Partner avatar icon (24px pill) placed top-right to clarify ownership at a glance.

### "Golden Window" Discovery Banner
- Pill or rounded-2xl banner with `#FEF3C7` background and `#F59E0B` hairline border.
- Features a sparkle micro-badge (`#D97706`), bold time duration ("Both free: Sat 2:00 PM – 7:30 PM"), and a quick-tap action ("Plan Something").

### Dual-User Timeline Column
- Side-by-side synchronized vertical tracks with hour tick marks running down the center gutter.
- Mutual free hours draw a unifying bridge spanning horizontally across both lanes, rendered in semi-transparent emerald with dashed outer borders.

### Date Picker Strip
- Horizontal swipeable row of vertical pill buttons (`rounded-full`, 44px wide × 64px tall).
- Selected day shifts to dark slate `#0F172A` with white typography, accompanied by a miniature dual-dot indicator showing both partners' shift statuses for that date.

### Inputs & Form Fields
- 52px height inputs with `rounded-xl` corners. Background `#FFFFFF` with `#E2E8F0` border.
- Focus state activates an emerald-tinted border `#10B981` with a `0 0 0 3px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.15)` focus ring.
